# BLU CITY levels
See `GAME_ARCHITECTURE.md` for the complete chapter, economy, equipment, sync and payment setup.

Six sequential missions now run on an expanded connected Central Grid. Existing version 2 local saves migrate to version 3 and continue at mission 3 when the original two missions are complete. Legacy Coin/BLU values remain intact locally. New chapter rewards total 1,230 Coin; replay does not duplicate them.

A repeatable Energy Circuit unlocks after mission 2. Equipment and dash can be bought with Coin earned in gameplay. Coin→BLU remains 100:1. Cloud account progress and purchases require the additive SQL migration; guest mode is local.
